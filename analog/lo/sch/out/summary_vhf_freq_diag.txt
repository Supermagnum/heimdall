## 8-phase requirement (re-ordered chain)

**Required:** VHF 8 phases (45°) for the harmonic-reject mixer.
**Source:** D4 (`decisions.md` 2026-10-19; `spec/phase2-plan.md` §5; this memo §1).
HR mixer status: Phase 3 still sizes the mixer's share; the ≥60 dB 3×LO target
including board preselection is in `spec/rf-frontend.md` §3 / `[DER]` §7.3 — D4
is not revoked.

**Can ÷2 → ÷3 → remaining ÷4 give the phases?**
- Total ratio ÷2×÷3×÷4 = ÷24 (correct).
- CMOS Johnson ÷4 on the ÷3 output gives **4 phases only** — fails D4.
- For **8 phases** with this order: clock a dual-edge / dual-rail ÷4 from a
  **50 %-duty** ÷3 at f_VCO/6 (rising → 4 phases, falling → interleaved 4),
  or equivalent. Cost [HAND]: ÷3 duty becomes a hard requirement (today D4
  makes ÷3 duty irrelevant); edge-matched dual counters; extra phase-error
  risk from rise/fall mismatch; more CMOS area/current. Not simulated here.

**÷3 at ≤ 2.1 GHz [SCH]:** tailed (TD) FAIL at all points at 1.08 and 1.05 V
despite buffer ≥0.54 V/side — still **headroom**. Tail-less (TL) PASS all
≤2.1 GHz at 1.08 and 1.05 V. Re-order alone does not revive the stacked latch.

**Recommendation: (b) folded PMOS-load ÷3.**
Reasons: (1) TD failure class is headroom at every frequency, including ≤2.1 GHz
with good swing — topology must unstack the latch. (2) Option (a) already failed
4.20 ss with B2=8 and production tank amp [SCH prior]; closing it needs tank swing
forbidden by D22, and VHF is already over D7. (3) Option (c) does not fix TD
headroom and breaks the simple D4 8-phase path unless dual-edge logic is added.

**Decision needed:** owner picks a / b / c (or hybrid). Do not implement here.
