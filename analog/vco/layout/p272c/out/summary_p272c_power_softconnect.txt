## Causes (not well/guard soft-connect)

These were **hard metal shorts from parent power pads**, not n-well / guard-ring
merges between neighbours.

1. **cmp ↔ vdd (ampcal_det)**  
   Cells: `ampcal_det` + top `vco_top` power routing.  
   Parent M2 H-pad for `ampcal_det.vdd` used column `det.right+3` (x≈339.6)
   with pad=4 µm, spanning x≈207→340 at y≈379–387. That pad covered child
   cmp Via1/M2 at (270.10, 385.29) [TOOL net tracer].  
   Fix: column `vdd_pin.x+8` (short stub); pad height 2 µm; drop east M2.b
   notch fill that sat on the old long H-pad.

2. **gnd ↔ vdd (tail_block)**  
   Cells: `tail_block` + top power.  
   Child has a vertical **gnd M2** at island x≈387 (y≈492–644) that crosses the
   vdd pin row. Parent M2 pad for `tail_block.vdd` overlapped that strip while
   Via1 tied parent M1 (on vdd pin) into the same M2 [TOOL; POWER_DROPS=
   `tail_block.vdd` alone reproduced `gnd|vdd`]. Leaf `tail_block` LVS has
   gnd and vdd separate [TOOL].  
   Fix: M1-only pin→column; M2/Via2 only at column east of the cell (no
   parent M2 over the cell east edge).
