# P2.7.3a VCO top extraction [PEX-RC]

## Method
- Tool: Magic 8.3 + `ihp-sg13g2.magicrc` via custom full-RC TCL (extresist threshold=100 mOhm, minres=50 mOhm, mindelay=0; cthresh 0.01; coupling on) [TOOL].
- GDS: `vco_top_lvs.gds` (sign-off assembly e0b5e5a, **pin-only** inductor). Fab `vco_top.gds` spiral would DC-short tn/tp in Magic [PEX-RC / TOOL].
- Inductor in sim: tapped L2n0 lumped EM `analog/vco/models/l2n0_lumped.inc` (via-fixed, mesh1 fit) on tn/tp/ct [EM].
- Varactor / MIM: Magic emits `cap_rfcmim` / `cap_cmim` as PDK devices; `sg13_hv_svaricap` appears as 2-terminal N-devices (incomplete pin list) [TOOL] — frequency decks use schematic PDK svaricap + extracted RC (see summary_pex_vco_range).
