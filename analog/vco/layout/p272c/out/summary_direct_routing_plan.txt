## Net corridors (every net its own)
| Net | Corridor |
|---|---|
| tn / tp | Separate y tracks on TM1 w=2; bank joins by **V stub only** at pin X through the 8 µm corridor (no west ring) |
| ct | Labelled LVS-checked top port (TM1); stub to det.ct |
| V_tune (vt) | Shielded M2 escape south of bank; no cross tn/tp |
| controls | South of bank / east of det; away from tn/tp |
| power | M2 H spines; unique M3 columns per pin |

## Join order
place → power → ct → tail → tn_tp → vt → controls
DRC + KLayout LVS + netgen after each; stop at first failure.
